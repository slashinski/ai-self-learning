# Module 4: Completion and Self-Assessment

*Estimated time: 3–4 hours*

---

## What This Module Covers

Module 4 is not about building new things. It is about proving that the things you built have changed how you work — and that you can articulate what you learned clearly enough to teach it.

There are three deliverables in this module, and none of them are technical:

1. **Behavior change reflection** — which of your artifacts are in your actual daily workflow, with evidence
2. **Handoff checklist review** — would a new developer be able to use your artifacts without you explaining them?
3. **Completion self-assessment** — a written evaluation of where you are against the four DLP Completion criteria

---

## The Difference Between Building and Adopting

You built seven delivery skills, one skill, a hook, a sentinel file, and a governed workflow. That is evidence of technical competency. It is not evidence of adoption.

Adoption means you reach for these tools instinctively — when you have a real task to do, you do not have to remind yourself to write the test first or structure your prompt as RTCC. You do it because it is your default.

The self-assessment in this module is designed to surface the honest answer to whether adoption has happened. If it has not yet happened, that is a finding — not a failure. The program is designed to produce adoption over time, through practice. What the self-assessment should not do is produce a document that looks like adoption without the underlying behavioral change.
